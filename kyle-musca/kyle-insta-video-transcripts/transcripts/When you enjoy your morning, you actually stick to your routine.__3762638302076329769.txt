So I lost 22 pounds of body fat and the biggest thing that helped me wasn't training harder or
eating cleaner. It was creating a morning routine that I actually enjoyed. Four years ago, I thought
being disciplined meant waking up at 5am and smashing a HIIT class before sunrise. But all
it was doing was burning me out. And honestly, it made me the type of person that just hated
fitness. The more I pushed and the more I sweat, the less progress I saw because I just couldn't
stay consistent. Now, my mornings look completely different. I start with a morning walk, grab a
coffee, then I fuel my body before a workout. And the focus of my workouts now is not to burn
calories or to get a sweat on or not punishing myself for what I ate on the weekend. Now, it's
about taking time for me and the habits that make me feel good and actually want to show up for
myself. Because when you enjoy your morning, you stay consistent. And when you're consistent,
the results come naturally. This is part seven of my Hope for Holidays series and in the next
part, I'll show you exactly how to maximize your recovery.

Four years ago, my boyfriend made me lose 22 pounds.
And it's not because he didn't love me.
It's because I wouldn't stop complaining.
I never felt overweight.
I just felt soft and uncomfortable.
Like that skinny fat feeling.
Honestly, I didn't know if I should focus on building muscle or losing fat.
So, I'd be stuck between the two, wanting bigger glutes, but smaller arms and defined abs.
I did more HIIT classes and cardio one week, then Pilates and random workouts the next week.
Only to look exactly the same months later.
I only started seeing real change when I started tracking my calories and following structured workouts focusing on progress.
Which is crazy because that's exactly what my boyfriend told me to do every time I complained about my back and my arms.
If you feel stuck like I did, you just need a clear plan to stick to.
Start with eating more protein.
With at least one gram per pound of your goal body weight.
I do four gym sessions a week, two upper, two lower and daily walks.
Anything more and I just wouldn't be consistent.
Once you commit to this, you can work out your exact calories and do the right workouts to see real progress.
But for now, just stop doing the same thing that's keeping you stuck and hoping for change.

If you have fat loss goals right now and you're stressing about undoing your progress over
Christmas, that was me for years, but now I enjoy food at Christmas while staying lean
and not stressing about it.
So I'm going to give you the exact plan I use at holiday time.
The first thing you need to understand is that Christmas is only three days out of 365.
A few days of celebrations is not enough to derail your progress, but that doesn't
mean you need to abandon your healthy habits either.
First, I increase my basics in the days leading up.
For steps, I'm aiming for 10 to 15K, consistent workouts, planning meals, and tracking macros.
This helps balance out the indulgent meals I know that are coming.
Then when it's Christmas day, I start with a high protein breakfast, get some movement
in, and stay hydrated.
I'm also prioritizing protein at lunch and dinner.
Roast meats, glazed ham, seafood, and this is the most important thing.
I indulge consciously.
I don't pick a food all day.
I choose a few desserts that I actually really want, and I enjoy without guilt because I
know I'm coming back to a plan that actually works.
These steps help me stop repeating the same cycle every single year.
Christmas is not a reason to panic about food if you know how to control yourself.

I'm going to tell you my secret to how I stopped binge eating, which helped me lose 18 pounds of
body fat. I used to feel completely out of control around food and I'd always turn to it for comfort
and saying no to a sweet treat felt impossible. But here's what changed. I realized that saying
no wasn't a punishment. It's just what needed to be done to see the results I wanted. I learned to
say no to certain foods so I could say yes to my goals later. But it wasn't about cutting everything
out forever. It just meant making intentional sacrifices. I also removed trigger foods from
my house because honestly, if I couldn't stop at one, it didn't belong in my kitchen and instead
started finding ways to satisfy my cravings that were a lot lower calorie and a lot healthier. Once
I started eating less sugar and processed foods, that light and energized feeling became more
addicting than the sugar itself. Removing yourself from temptations doesn't have to be a bad thing.
Sometimes it's just necessary. Then once I reached my goals, I was able to add back in the things
that I loved in moderation. And now I've just learned when to have them and when to say no.

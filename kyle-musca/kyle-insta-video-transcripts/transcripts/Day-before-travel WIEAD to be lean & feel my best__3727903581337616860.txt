This is everything I eat in a day before a trip to feel lean, toned, and confident.
The biggest mistake I see women making is panicking and slashing calories right before
they leave. But the look you take on holiday is from weeks and months of structure beforehand,
not last minute. That's why I always keep my meals nutrient-dense, focusing on the exact
same foods that I always have, and focusing on high-protein, nutrient-dense foods that
won't play with my digestion and leave me feeling bloated and puffy the next day.

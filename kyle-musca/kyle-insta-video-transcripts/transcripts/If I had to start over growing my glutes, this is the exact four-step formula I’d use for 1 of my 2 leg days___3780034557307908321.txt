If I had to start over to grow my glutes, I would follow this exact four-step workout formula for one of my two leg days.
Step one, we're going to pick a compound exercise like hip thrust.
Set your upper back on the bench, keep your ribs down, shins vertical, and drive through your heels.
Step two, our second lift, we're going to do 45-degree hip extensions.
Hinge at the hips, think of driving your hips into the pad, keep your core tight, squeeze at the top, and don't rush these reps.
Step three, we want to be picking a unilateral movement so you're really targeting each glute individually.
Lean slightly forward, plant your whole foot on the box, and drive up through your working leg.
Step four, you're going to pick another isolation movement.
I'm picking abductions so I can grow that upper shelf.

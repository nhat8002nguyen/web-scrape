four years ago my boyfriend told me i was going about my fatless journey all wrong i was picking
small random timelines six eight twelve weeks just because it sounded right not because it was right
for my goals and the funny thing was i never even made it to the end of the time because i was
trying every crash diet to get me there ended up just making me confused and frustrated on why i
couldn't stay consistent until i realized he was right all along i should have just focused on what
was actually sustainable from the start from the very beginning i was setting myself up for failure
with extreme diets and deficit that i would almost immediately abandon after only a few
weeks instead of creating a lifestyle i could live with and reversing out once i was happy with my
result because the truth is the amount of time it takes is different for everyone your deficit
should last as long as it takes for you to feel lean toned and confident i made mine something i
could stick to focusing on high protein choosing meals i actually enjoyed and aiming for balance
instead of restriction i've lost 22 pounds but more importantly i stopped feeling trapped by
diet now i can diet maintain and reverse without bouncing because i've learned how to do it the
right way

I've been having steak for dinner every day for the past two years, which is crazy because I used
to hate it, but now it is honestly my favorite meal of the day. It's been the easiest way to
hit my protein since losing 22 pounds. And from cooking it almost every day for myself and my
partner for two years, I can honestly say I know how to cook a tasty steak. A step that you really
don't want to skip is leaving your steak out for at least 20 to 30 minutes. Then you want to season
generously. I use lots of salt, garlic, paprika, and magic dust. Make sure the pan is super hot,
then sear the fat first. Then you can just cook it in the fat without adding extra oil. Optionally,
you can add a little bit of extra garlic and herb. For medium rare, you want to make sure that
the blood is coming through the top first before taking it off the pan, but that's always going to
get you a perfect steak. If you don't have your steak medium rare, you can unfollow. Honestly,
so delicious. You could not catch me eating chicken and broccoli.

If I wake up 22 pounds heavier like I was 5 years ago, this is exactly what I would do to lose it again.
First of all, nothing is going to happen without a calorie deficit, so make sure you're in one.
Now that we've figured out we're in a calorie deficit, we need to know how to actually feel those calories properly
because there's no point in grabbing a handful of nuts when you feel hungry.
That's not going to get you the results that you want if you want a lean tone physique.
So, if I didn't want to feel like I was absolutely starving all day and actually hating my life,
I would fill my calories with high-protein, nutrient-dense meals.
Not only that, especially in the beginning of this calorie deficit,
I would allow myself a treat once a week or allow myself to enjoy my favorite foods
so I don't crash out and burn out.
I would also make sure I know exactly how to use my fitness pal and track everything.
At the end of the day, if you're just guessing your way through this,
you don't know if you're actually going to see results or not.
I would also stop being afraid of the scale and start weighing myself weekly
so that I know if what I'm doing is actually working.
And the last thing I would do is be patient because you cannot skip the process.

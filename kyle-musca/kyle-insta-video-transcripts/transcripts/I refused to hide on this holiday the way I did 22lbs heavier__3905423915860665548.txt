After feeling this good in a backless dress, I refused to go back to the girl that was 22 pounds heavier because she would spend hours picking an outfit just to end up hating how she looks and choosing the cover-up anyway.
And I stayed like that for three years because I was convinced lifting would grow the fat I was already insecure about.
So, naturally, I did the opposite. Extra cardio, Pilates, lighter weights, and restricting food.
And I still looked the same.
And after coaching over 2,000 women, I know the worst part isn't the dress.
The nights you spend the whole dinner thinking about how you look.
But the fix is actually simpler than you think.
Because what you're seeing isn't bulky muscle.
Body fat sitting on top of a back with no shape underneath.
Which is why, even when you lose the fat, you still feel like you need to lose more.
The only way to change this is building muscle and losing the fat at the same time.
So, here's how you can do it fast.
Start training your back as hard as you train your glutes.
Focus on underhand pull-downs, rows of rope-face pulls.
Find the weight you fail at and try to beat it every week.
While you build it, eat in a real calorie deficit.
While actually tracking, not just eating clean.
Weigh in once a week and confirm your weight is dropping over a two-week window.
Deficit without back training gives you smaller softness.
To back and back training without a deficit grows the muscle under the fat.
You need both.
Then you'll start wearing the expensive dresses you actually packed for.

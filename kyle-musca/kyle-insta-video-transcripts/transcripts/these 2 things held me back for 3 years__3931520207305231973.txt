Eating healthy kept me soft and bloated for three years. I wanted a toned stomach so bad I would
load up on the vegetables, eat all the salads, and somehow got more bloated and more uncomfortable
in my own body. And after coaching 2,000 women, I realized I wasn't the only one stuck in this
exact cycle. Firstly, I had to realize more green things doesn't equal flatter stomach.
I was eating giant salads loaded with nuts, seeds, and dressing, thinking I'd be losing fat,
but then ending up looking six months pregnant after every meal. But probably thinking,
okay, cool, cut fiber, and you couldn't be more wrong. Second thing was figuring out what my body
actually needed. All of the healthy extras like the nuts, the seeds, the oils. Well, yes, they're
great for your health, but for my physique goals, they were holding me back. As someone with a
petite frame and low muscle mass and not moving as much as I thought I was, I realized I actually
needed to be eating a lot less. So here's what finally worked. Switching to whole foods that
were easily digestible for my body. Focusing on one protein, I was able to eat a lot less.
One protein, one fat, and one carb at each meal, and opting for more warm meals of a huge raw
cruciferous salad. Still including fiber, just not going so hard and so fast that my gut couldn't
keep up. Do this for seven days and watch how much tighter and flatter your stomach feels.

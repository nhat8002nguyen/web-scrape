I want to grow my glutes but I want to keep my legs slim so this is one of the workouts I've
been doing. Starting off with cable abduction. It's honestly such a hack for growing the upper
glute and getting that rounded look. And doing these first means it's a lot easier to build
this muscle without the fatigue of the rest of the workout. And these burn. Next up is hip thrust and
this is my main heavy lift. Here I'm trying to focus on full range slow eccentrics and pausing
at the top and bottom of every single rep. The slow and controlled movement on the way down
and the pauses are non-negotiable if you want to build that shell. Then I'm hitting 45 degree
hip extensions. Isolate the glutes perfectly. I focus on pushing my hips into the pad as I drive
my way up and squeezing the glutes hard but keeping the tension the whole way through. Then lastly is
Bulgarian split squats and yes these suck. They're insane for the glutes. Lean slightly forward and
drive up through your heels. As always keeping it slow and controlled and going deep to hit the
glutes properly not just the quads.

four years ago my boyfriend told me i was doing my back workouts all wrong and it took me months
to realize he was right i was copying random tiktok workouts chasing that burn with hundreds
of light reps thinking that's the only way to turn your back no matter how much time i spent
in the gym my back looked exactly the same i finally realized he was right all along i needed
to focus on form and exercise selection not just doing more reps so i started training back
intentionally and with purpose each session included underhand lap pull down single arm
breathe out flies and a couple of tricep movements to balance my upper body i slowed
down every rep and focused on progressive overload also paired that with a calorie
deficit now my back is stronger more defined but more importantly i finally feel confident
in backless dresses and tops i would have never have touched four years ago

let me tell you a secret that i feel like nobody really talks about i actually saw the most progress
when i started working out less i know that sounds crazy i used to train six to seven days a week i'd
push through feeling exhausted and burnt out because i seriously thought that's what you were
supposed to do but i was literally just over training my body was constantly inflamed i was
sore all the time and nothing was changing i even felt more puffy once i started going to the gym
less weight literally just dropped off i started going four days a week two upper two lower actually
resting just walking and eating more my strength went up my shape changed i started sleeping
properly and my body finally started responding the key really is to train with intention do the
right movements give your body time to recover and you'll finally start seeing the results you've
been chasing

I've been craving the viral chicken curry but I wanted to make it fit my calories so that's
exactly what I did and it almost tastes identical to the real thing but even better and tastier
because I decided to fry the chicken instead of poaching it. It takes half the time and makes
half the mess. This whole meal only took me 20 minutes. You can serve this with jasmine rice
or even potatoes but I chose to use cauliflower rice because it was lower calories and a lot
quicker. The flavors were all there, rich, spicy, sweet and so satisfying. You don't have
to give up your favorite foods just to lose fat, you just have to make smarter swaps.

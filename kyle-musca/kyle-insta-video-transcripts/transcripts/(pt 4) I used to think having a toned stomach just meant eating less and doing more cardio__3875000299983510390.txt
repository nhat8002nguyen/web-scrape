I have a toned stomach, but I didn't always look like this
and it wasn't just eating less that fixed it.
Four years ago, I didn't even know
this was possible for me.
I was bloated, puffy, inflamed,
but according to the internet, I was doing everything right.
I was eating healthy, doing my steps, doing my cardio,
but my body still looked soft.
After coaching over 2,000 ladies,
I realized we were all stuck in the same cycle,
chasing fat loss instead of a better looking body.
And that may sound contradicting, so let me explain.
Here are the three mistakes we were all guilty of.
Mistake number one,
thinking fat loss automatically means tone.
Yes, eating less makes you smaller,
but without muscle, it also makes you softer.
Mistake number two, living in a permanent diet
and never doing a proper body recomposition phase.
Eating in a deficit, high protein, and weightlifting.
When I say weightlifting,
I don't just mean lifting a weight,
I mean actual strength progress.
Which brings me to mistake number three,
obsessing over the scale instead of focusing on strength.
Now, I don't mean the scale is bad.
It's actually a great tool for measuring progress.
Your body can lose fat and build muscle at the same time.
Photos and how your clothes fit
are gonna tell a way better story
because muscle is dense and tight and fat is fluffy.
So now what do you focus on?
Well, here's the simple tone tummy formula I use.
Moderate calorie deficit,
three to four heavy lifts a week,
daily steps, consistent protein target for every day.
Don't need to shrink yourself
in order to get a toned stomach.
Do this for the next few weeks
and watch how much tighter your body gets.

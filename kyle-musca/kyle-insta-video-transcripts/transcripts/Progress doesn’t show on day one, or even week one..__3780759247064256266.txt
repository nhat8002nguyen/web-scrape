This is just a reminder, if you're trying to lose weight or get healthy, you have to
be okay with going through a phase where you're doing everything right and still seeing no
change.
This is completely normal.
It doesn't mean that you're failing or the plan isn't working.
If you're following your plan consistently, you know, you're tracking your weights, you're
hitting your protein, you're tracking your progress, the changes are not always going
to show immediately and that is why people quit because they don't see it fast enough.
If you just give up every time it feels hard or it's starting to get uncomfortable,
you're going to be forever stuck in the cycle of quitting too soon, starting over and never
seeing the results that you want.
The key really is pushing through those hard moments and being able to feel okay with being
uncomfortable and if you can finally push through that, you're going to come out on
the other side with the results that you want.

I have a toned back now, but I actually used to be very insecure about how it looked.
It's just wearing naturally stored more body fat.
And even though I was working out every day, it still stayed the same for years.
I hated wearing certain tops.
I wore baggy tees to the gym.
And I avoided wearing backwards dresses.
But after coaching over 2000, I realized these are the same five traps we were all falling into.
And I personally fell into every single one of them.
Trap one, thinking your back looks big because of muscle.
Trust me, girl, it is not muscle.
It's fat sitting on top of an untrained back.
So you actually need to do both at once.
Lose the fat in a real tracked calorie deficit and build the muscle underneath.
One without the other keeps you soft.
Trap two, blaming your hormones for the puffy look.
Look, I get it.
Everyone online loves to use the C word as the main problem.
And I've believed this for years too.
But after coaching so many ladies, nine times out of ten, it's calories, not cortisol.
Trap number three, trying to tone or spot reduce the fat with light weights and high reps.
Trust me, it's not doing the work that you think it is.
You need to pick up real weights and push yourself to the point where your body actually wants to change.
Trap four, avoiding training back altogether.
Because you don't want to get bulky.
I get it because I used to think my HIIT cardio is burning more fat too.
But now I can confidently say, training your back is the secret to getting the tight, snatched look.
Train it two to three times a week in the beginning.
A stronger back is what's going to make your waist look smaller, your posture better, and your upper body more feminine.
And the last trap, trying to hate yourself into changing.
No amount of negative self-talk and pinching in the mirror is going to make the body fat come off any faster.
It just causes you daily stress and makes the process feel slower.
When you start acting like the woman that already has the back you want, that's when the real change starts.
I wasted years stuck in these.
So don't be like me and focus on walking, weights, and the right nutrition.
You'll finally get the back you want.

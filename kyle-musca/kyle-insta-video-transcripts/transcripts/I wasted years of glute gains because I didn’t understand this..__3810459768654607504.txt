I've been going to the gym for 10 years now
and it genuinely annoys me how much glute growth
I left on the table because I didn't understand this.
And if you're training, hitting your protein
and doing hip thrusts and your glutes still aren't growing,
I know how frustrating it is.
I thought I was training hard
because my workouts were burning.
But what I didn't realize back then
is that feeling tired or feeling the burn
is not the same as training close to failure.
I was just stopping at the burn
without realizing those are the reps that count the most.
So what does failure mean then?
It means you cannot physically do one more rep.
Like you cannot lift that off the ground.
And that amount of effort actually signals growth.
Once I started doing this,
my glutes finally started to grow.
And it's the same thing I see with the girls I coach
who swear they've tried everything.
When they actually start pushing themselves,
they see results.

I get asked a lot what my cardio routine is because I was able to lose fat and get relatively toned.
I believe that less is more when combining cardio with weight training,
and I also know from experience you can achieve the results you want with minimal cardio.
So I'm going to show exactly what I do for cardio.
Basically, I work a desk job from home.
The only movement I do throughout the day is really my weighted sessions.
So I try to make up for this by starting my day with a 10 to 20 minute walk.
Then after my gym session, I'll also try and do a 10 or 15 minute walk on the treadmill,
or if I'm feeling brave, it'll be on the stairs.
Then at the end of the day, if I have time, I'll try and get another 10 minute walk in.
And on the days that I don't go to the gym, I'll go for a 40 minute walk outside, and that's it.

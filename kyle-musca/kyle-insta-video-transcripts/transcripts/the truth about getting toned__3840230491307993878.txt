Okay, real quick. This is what I used to look like when I was doing HIIT workouts four times a week.
I was killing myself on the Stairmaster and I was doing a boxing class.
I was also trying so hard to try to lose the fat. This is me now.
And what's crazy to me is that now I do so much less. I don't do HIIT classes anymore.
I don't go to the gym six to seven times a week. I'm not killing myself on the Stairmaster every single day.
I'm not running. I have so much more energy and so much more time on my hands.
All I do now is my four weight sessions and walking for cardio.
And in my opinion, yes, I look so much better. My muscles are way more defined.
It looks like I actually go to the gym. I am so much more healthier now that I've learned exactly how to hit my protein,
how to fuel my body. Because the thing is, more effort does not always equal better results.
You do not need to be doing hours and hours of cardio to look lean and toned.
You need to simplify your routine, focus on weight training, walking and proper nutrition,
and your body will finally start responding the way you want it to.

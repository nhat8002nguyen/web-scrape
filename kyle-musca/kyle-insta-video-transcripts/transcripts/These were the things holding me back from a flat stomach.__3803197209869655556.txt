You're probably a girl that wants a flat stomach. I'm a coach and this is the number one thing women
ask me about. I didn't always have a flat stomach and visible abs, so I'm going to tell you exactly
what I changed to get there. One of the biggest changes was eating proper meals instead of just
grazing all the time. I used to think smaller snacks were helping because I wasn't getting
that full feeling, but all that did was keep my intake high without ever feeling satisfied. Once
I prioritized solid meals with enough protein, the snacking naturally dropped off. I also stopped
telling myself certain foods didn't count. The little taste while cooking, bites off my fiance's
plate, extra sauces, just a few chocolates after dinner, pretty much pretending it didn't exist if I
didn't track it. You don't want to track the small stuff because you think it doesn't count, but it
does. And if you've noticed that so far all I've talked about is nutrition, that's because they're
not wrong when they say abs are made in the kitchen. Nutrition really is a priority and you
have to make sure you're in a calorie deficit if you want to lose belly fat. While I was making
sure I was in a consistent calorie deficit, I was also training my abs because once the fat's gone
you need something to show. But the biggest difference was my mindset. I stopped treating
fat loss like it was something I needed to rush and started treating it like a lifestyle. That's
when you stop looking at it and you're like, oh my god, I'm going to lose weight. I'm going to lose
every day and it naturally just happens.

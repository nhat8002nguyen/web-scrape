What's really the difference between cardio and weight training?
Cardio helps with fat loss and overall health, but weight training builds muscle which burns
calories even at rest. You might have heard cardio is great for burning fat stores in zone 2,
but without weight training it will not get you that toned body you're looking for. You end up
looking small but soft and puffy. Weight training builds muscle, supports bone and joint health,
increases your metabolism, improves your mental health, physical health and your sleep, plus keep
you looking hot when you're 80. I know this because 4 years ago I was focusing more on
cardio than weights, wondering why I still look soft. I wanted a toned strong body so I changed
everything. Weights 4 times a week with progressive overload. I swapped HIIT cardio and running with
walking. This combo literally shapes your body while keeping you lean, strong and feminine.

This is the only guide you'll need to get rid of skinny fat and actually get lean,
toned and shredded. Skinny fat is where you have high body fat percentage and low muscle mass.
You're not overweight just lacking the muscle definition that gives you shape and confidence.
This is me here and exactly what I did to fix it. Firstly, I stopped overdoing the cardio in
high intensity workouts. Cardio burns calories but it does not build shape. I was doing way
too much stressing out my body and losing muscle. So I cut it back and focused on strength instead.
Next, I ate in a calorie deficit with high protein. Meaning I ate enough to stay fueled
and recover properly but kept calories low enough to actually lose body fat. I started
lifting only four times a week and actually trained hard. Focusing on progressive overload,
meaning I track my lifts and focused on improving each week. And that's what built definition and
curves in all the right places. And if you do all these things consistently, you'll lose the
soft look and actually build the toned strong body you've been chasing.

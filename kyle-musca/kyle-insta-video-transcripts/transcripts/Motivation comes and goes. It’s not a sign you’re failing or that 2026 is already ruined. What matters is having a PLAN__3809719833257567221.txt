I don't know who needs to hear this, but if you're relying on motivation to achieve your New Year's goals,
you'll probably be a part of the 90% of people who fail by February.
And if your motivation already feels lower, you are not alone.
This happens to almost everyone.
You start January fired up, routines feel exciting, you think this year feels different.
Then in just a few weeks, what gets busy, plans pop up, you miss a workout or your eat-off plan once,
and suddenly motivation is gone.
But this is totally normal.
And it doesn't mean you lack discipline or this year is already ruined.
You need to understand that motivation is just a feeling, it comes and it goes.
What actually matters is having a plan that works even when you don't feel like it.
So here's the secret.
Have simple workouts you can do on low-energy days.
Build habits small enough that you can build them into your daily routine.
And focus on progress from repetition, not intensity.
And if this already feels harder than you expected, don't worry.
If you're not failing, you're just at the exact point where real consistency starts.

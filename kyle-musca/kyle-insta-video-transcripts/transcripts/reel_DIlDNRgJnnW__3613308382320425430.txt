How do I get slim and toned legs is a common question I always get and the answer is probably
not what you want to hear but is true for most of you. If there's fat covering your legs,
no special workout or special exercise is going to make them look lean. You need to be in a calorie
deficit. That's what reveals definition. We all know you can't spot reduced fat and we genetically
can't pick where it comes from first. So you need to stay consistent in a deficit until your body
leans out overall. But that's not all. If you want long, lean and defined legs, you need to
stop being afraid of weights. Using challenging weights is not going to make you look bulky,
it's going to tighten and tone 10 times faster than your bodyweight exercise. And no, you can't
avoid quad exercises if you want nice glutes. Just drop the isolation ones like leg extensions
and focus on the compound movements. Then all you need is daily walks. Underrated but one of
the best ways to burn fat consistently without burning yourself. So stop overcomplicating it,
eat in a deficit, lift heavy, walk daily and stop blaming your genetics.

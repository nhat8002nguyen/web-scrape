You've been lied to because one weekend of drinking and overeating can actually derail your fat loss progress.
I drink alcohol on the weekend and every time I do, I'm reminded why I barely drink anymore.
It's crazy because I used to drink every single weekend and I genuinely believe that if I just showed up to the gym and ate healthy Monday to Friday, I would get my dream body.
And honestly, it's not even the drinks that do the real damage.
It was the days following where I was tired, unmotivated, craving everything that was not on my meal plan.
So the weekend just gone, I had burgers, pasta, and half a tub of Ben & Jerry's, not even because I was hungry, but because I was craving that dopamine.
My discipline had completely vanished.
That's when I realized why my progress used to feel so impossible.
Not because I was doing anything wrong during the week, but because I was resetting every weekend.
Now I save alcohol for special moments because waking up clear and in control feels better than any cocktail.
So yes, one weekend can ruin your progress if you let it drag on through the week.
So just get back on your routine, hydrate, move, eat well, and don't let one slip into a whole week.

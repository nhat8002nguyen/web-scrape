I have a toned stomach but I didn't always look like this and it wasn't more crunches that fixed
it. Four years ago I never thought this was possible. I was puffy, bloated, uncomfortable
and doing ab workouts every single day and my midsection still looked soft. After coaching
over 2,000 ladies I realized we were all making the same three mistakes. Mistake number one,
thinking more ab exercises will burn your belly fat. Your abs are a muscle. Now squishy stuff on
top is your belly fat. This will only come off if your whole body is in a calorie deficit. So
you're smashing yourself in the gym every day just to undo it on the weekends with extra drinks
and snacks and your weekly average calories are way higher than you think. Mistake number two,
living off low protein and low fiber food. They keep you hungry and craving more food so you're
never able to stick to your deficit. Once we move our girls to three high protein meals per day with
a planned treat, the cravings drop and suddenly their lower belly starts tightening up. Mistake
number three, ignoring steps and stressing about the scale. A toned stomach loves steps and sleep
way more than another HIIT class. And don't worry, I'm not going to leave you with
that actionable step. So here's the super simple toned tummy formula I use. 10k steps a day,
three to four weight sessions a week, and most meals cooked at home with at least 20 to 30 grams
of protein. And you're probably thinking this sounds way too easy. You would be right, my
future toned midsection sister, because the hard part is not what you have to do. The hard part is
sticking to it. You just need the right non-negotiables to stick to long term. Do this
and your stomach will be tightening up before you even add a new gym session.

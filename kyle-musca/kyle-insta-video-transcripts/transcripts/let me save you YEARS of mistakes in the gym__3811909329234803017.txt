These are all the mistakes I've made that held me back for years in the gym from seeing the
results I wanted and I'm still seeing them today so I want to save you the years that I wasted.
First mistake was completely restricting my food to the point of basically nothing
because I thought you had to eat little. All that did was make me starving by night time,
I would raid the cupboard and on the weekends I had zero self-control so obviously nothing stuck.
Next thing, I thought I had to be starving in between my meals to be able to lose fat.
Yes, there is a degree where you have to be hungry in order to lose body fat in a calorie
deficit but I was absolutely ravenous. I wasn't losing body fat because when I was allowed to eat,
I wouldn't be able to stop. My third mistake was avoiding weights out of fear of getting bulky
and I was surprised when I wasn't getting the muscle tone that I wanted. Fourth, I was switching
out my workouts every few weeks. I wouldn't stick to it for more than two weeks because I would
just get bored and I thought the exercises weren't working because I didn't have a dump
truck in two weeks but unfortunately, it's the boring stuff that actually gets you to where
you want to go and if you're doing all of these things and you haven't seen
results yet, that's probably why.

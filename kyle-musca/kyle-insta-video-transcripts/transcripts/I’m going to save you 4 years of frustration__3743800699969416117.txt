Four years ago, my boyfriend told me I was going about my fitness journey all wrong by avoiding heavy weights.
And it took me two years to realize he was right.
I listened to the internet advice and did more cardio and ate basically nothing.
And it still didn't help me lose the extra fat.
And then I finally realized my boyfriend was right.
I should have just picked up the weights from the start.
Before that, I was stuck in that skinny fat look and doing all of the wrong workouts.
I had no idea what I was doing.
But once I started lifting, especially on back days, that's when everything changed.
I stopped just chasing smaller and started getting stronger.
So not only by lifting weights did I start creating more shape and definition,
I started burning more fat and calories at rest.
Since then, I've lost 22 pounds.
But most importantly, I feel confident.
And finally, wear the clothes that I wouldn't have dared to try on four years ago.

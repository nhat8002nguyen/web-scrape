I wish somebody told me sooner when I was trying to get lean and toned that it's not just about
eating less, it's about what you eat. If your meals don't balance your blood sugar, you will
constantly feel tired, hungry, and crave even more sugar no matter how disciplined you are,
which is honestly a nightmare when you're trying to lose body fat and be in a calorie deficit.
That's why every meal I eat, I try to focus on protein, fats, and fiber to allow my body to
build lean muscle and keep me full for hours. Once I started creating balanced meals, being
in a calorie deficit honestly felt so easy. I've had more energy, fewer cravings, and have
been able to keep a lean and toned physique for years.

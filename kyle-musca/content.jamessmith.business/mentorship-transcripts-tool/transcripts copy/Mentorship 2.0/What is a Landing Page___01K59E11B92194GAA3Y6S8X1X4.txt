A landing page is a single web page designed with one goal
in mind, to get a visitor to take a specific action. Unlike
a normal website which may have multiple pages and
distractions, a landing page is focused and direct, usually
built for things like collecting leads, selling a product or
getting signups. The key difference is that every element of
the page is designed to guide the visitor towards a single
outcome without giving them a bunch of other options to
click on. Now what is it used for? Landing pages are
marketing tools. Businesses use them when they want people
to do something specific. Whether that's buying a product,
maybe unfortunately booking a call, signing up for a
webinar, downloading an ebook or subscribing to a
newsletter. But the newsletter will technically put under a
